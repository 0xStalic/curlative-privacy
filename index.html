Operation Nightfall: Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026

Operation Nightfall is made by Curlative Development ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what choices you have. It covers the game on Steam (Windows), Android and iOS, including the free demo.

In short: there are no ads and no tracking, and we don't sell anything about you. Your progress stays on your device (or in Steam Cloud). The only things that leave it are what's needed for achievements, the online leaderboards and purchases, and anonymous crash reports.

What the game keeps on your device

Your progress, mission records, settings and control bindings are saved on your own device. We never receive them. If you play on Steam, Steam Cloud may copy your save to your Steam account so it follows you between PCs; that's handled by Valve.

Online leaderboards (challenge mode)

Challenge mode has online leaderboards. They are optional: the rest of the game works without them.

  • On Steam: your score is posted to Steam's leaderboards under your Steam account and Steam name. Your Steam friends and other players can see it. Valve runs this service.
  • On Android and iOS: the game signs in to Unity Gaming Services anonymously. This creates a random player ID on your device; there's no account, email or password. When you post a score, the leaderboard stores that player ID, your score, and the name you choose (or one generated for you). Other players can see your name and score. Unity, our service provider, runs this service and processes the data needed to run it, such as your IP address.

Please don't use your real name or anything personal as your leaderboard name.

Achievements and presence (Steam)

On Steam, the game unlocks Steam achievements and shows what you're doing in the game (for example, "Infiltrating Pier Nine") to your Steam friends. Valve handles this under your Steam account settings.

Purchases (Android and iOS)

The full game is a one-time in-app purchase. Google Play or the App Store handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details. The game only receives confirmation that the purchase went through, so it can unlock the full game and restore it later.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, the Unity engine may send an anonymous crash report to Unity. This includes technical details such as the device model, operating system and what went wrong. It doesn't include your name or contact details. We use these reports only to fix bugs.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking to follow what you do in the game.
  • We don't sell, rent or trade any information.
  • We don't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or photos.

Children

Operation Nightfall contains violence and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has posted to a leaderboard, contact us and we'll remove it.

How long we keep it

Leaderboard entries stay on the leaderboard until the board is reset or you ask us to remove them. Daily challenge boards reset every day. Crash reports are kept by Unity under its own retention policy.

Your rights and choices

  • Don't use the online leaderboards if you'd rather not share a score and name. Everything else in the game works offline.
  • Ask us to delete your leaderboard entries. On Android and iOS, tell us your leaderboard name and roughly when you posted, and we'll delete your player's data from Unity Gaming Services. On Steam, you can manage your data through your Steam account.
  • Ask us about your data. If you're in the UK or the EU, you have rights under data protection law (UK GDPR / GDPR), including to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us using the address below.

Services we use

These providers process data on our behalf or under your account with them. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services, crash reports): https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
